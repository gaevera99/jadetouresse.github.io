01 / ABOUT

About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Durham University. My research focuses on solar jets and magnetic switchbacks, with a particular interest in the role of solar jets in the formation and propagation of switchbacks in the solar wind.

I completed my PhD at the Laboratoire de Physique des Plasmas (LPP), Sorbonne Université, under the supervision of Étienne Pariat (FSLAC) and Clara Froment (LPC2E). My doctoral research investigated the propagation of solar jets as a possible solar origin scenario for magnetic switchbacks.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

MHD simulations of solar jets

I use magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) simulations to investigate the dynamics and propagation of solar jets. My work focuses on understanding the physical processes that govern jet evolution and their potential role in transporting energy and magnetic structures away from the Sun.

Switchbacks

I investigate the possible connection between solar jets and switchbacks observed in the solar wind. In particular, I explore and compare the signatures given by a synthetic in situ probe through a solar jet with observations by NASA’s Parker Solar Probe.

Connecting Solar Activity to the Solar Wind

I use in-situ data from Parker Solar Probe and solar observations from THEMIS to help interpret simulation results and explore possible links between processes at the Sun and structures observed in the solar wind.
